# Study of conditional frequency

    Hypotesis: given an considerable big dataset with n attributs per row, it's possible by isolating
the m most frequent attributs (with m < n), to detect if the row of data overflows the margin of error.

    Interest: In the discution of Anomaly Detection, an possible approach of analysis is the comparation between the average frequency of the sockets attributes and the error threshold defined by it history
